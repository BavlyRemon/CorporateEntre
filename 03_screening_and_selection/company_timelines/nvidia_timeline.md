# Nvidia Timeline

Working strategic eras and inflection points for CE/innovation analysis.

## 1993-1999

NVIDIA is founded by Jensen Huang and others; official company biography links the 1999 GPU invention to graphics and later parallel computing.

## 2006-2016

CUDA and accelerated computing reposition GPUs beyond gaming toward scientific computing, cloud, and deep learning.

## 2020

Mellanox/data-center expansion strengthens NVIDIA's platform/ecosystem logic.

## 2023-2024

Generative AI demand turns accelerated computing into a broad platform shift with major supply, ecosystem, and execution implications.
