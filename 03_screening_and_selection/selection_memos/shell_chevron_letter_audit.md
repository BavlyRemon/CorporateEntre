# Shell And Chevron Letter Audit

Access dates: 2026-04-16 and 2026-04-18.

## Bottom Line

Shell is suitable for CEO-review analysis using the official annual-report PDFs already in the workspace. The selected Shell reports contain explicit "Chair's message" and/or "Chief Executive Officer's review" sections.

Chevron needed correction. The earlier workspace relied on Chevron SEC 10-K text for selected years, which did not contain stockholder-letter language. I rechecked official Chevron annual-report PDF URLs and downloaded the selected annual reports. The selected Chevron years now have official annual-report PDFs with "to our stockholders" letter markers.

The final Shell and Chevron years are not the oldest possible years. That is deliberate. The close-reading design now uses seven years per company and prioritizes strategic inflection points within a comparable contemporary renewal window rather than a full corporate-history sample. Older Shell and Chevron years are documented as archival robustness candidates where they would be useful for background, but they are not the strongest seven-letter set for the CE/innovation comparison.
