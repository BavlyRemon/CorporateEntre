# Shell Selection Rationale

Final selected years: 2014, 2015, 2016, 2020, 2022, 2024, 2025.

The Shell set now contains seven report-years. The selected source is the official annual report and accounts PDF for each year, using the Chair's message and/or Chief Executive Officer's review as the strategic leadership text. The expanded set strengthens the defensibility of the energy comparison because it includes a pre-BG baseline, the BG acquisition year, post-BG integration, COVID/dividend reset, Shell plc and energy-security shock, and the mature Wael Sawan-era capital-discipline period.

## Selected Years

### 2014 - Pre-BG baseline

2014 is selected because it gives a clean pre-BG baseline under Ben van Beurden. It lets the later analysis distinguish Shell's pre-acquisition strategic language from the more explicit portfolio-renewal language in 2015 and the post-transaction integration language in 2016. Source: Shell Annual Report and Accounts 2014, official Shell archive, accessed 2026-04-16.

### 2015 - BG acquisition and low-price reset

2015 is selected because it captures strategic portfolio renewal under energy-market pressure. The official Shell annual report contains a Chief Executive Officer's review and identifies Ben van Beurden as Chief Executive Officer. It is a strong Corporate Entrepreneurship text because it combines acquisition logic, capital discipline, external turbulence, and long-term portfolio choice. Source: Shell Annual Report and Accounts 2015, official Shell archive, accessed 2026-04-16.

### 2016 - Post-BG integration

2016 is selected because it shows what happens after the major strategic choice. It is useful for comparing strategy-as-choice with strategy-as-execution: integration, simplification, capital discipline, and portfolio follow-through. In CE terms, it helps separate option selection from agile execution and organizational discipline. Source: Shell Annual Report and Accounts 2016, official Shell archive, accessed 2026-04-16.

### 2020 - COVID, dividend reset, and energy transition

2020 is selected because it captures crisis plus strategic renewal. The official Shell annual report contains both Chair's message and Chief Executive Officer's review sections, with Ben van Beurden identified as Chief Executive Officer. This year is important for disruption, resilience, capital allocation, energy transition, and organizational response under pressure. Source: Shell Annual Report and Accounts 2020, official Shell archive, accessed 2026-04-16.

### 2022 - Shell plc and energy-security shock

2022 is selected because it captures governance/identity shift plus Russia/energy-market disruption. The official Shell annual report contains Chair's message and Chief Executive Officer's review sections and documents the transition from Ben van Beurden to Wael Sawan. It is analytically useful for purpose/governance, geopolitical horizon scanning, and transition-era strategic renewal. Source: Shell Annual Report and Accounts 2022, official Shell archive, accessed 2026-04-16.

### 2024 - Capital discipline and transition pragmatism

2024 is selected because it captures mature reinvention under investor and policy pressure. The official Shell annual report contains Chair's message and Chief Executive Officer's review sections, with Wael Sawan identified as Chief Executive Officer. It is valuable for studying how Shell narrates capital discipline, LNG/core strengths, transition choices, and shareholder returns. Source: Shell Annual Report and Accounts 2024, official Shell archive, accessed 2026-04-16.

### 2025 - Latest Sawan-era execution

2025 is selected because the seven-letter requirement benefits from the latest official Shell strategic text. It extends the Sawan-era comparison and provides current language on capital discipline, LNG, portfolio focus, transition pragmatism, and performance. It should be treated as a current strategic narrative rather than evidence of final strategic outcomes. Source: Shell Annual Report and Accounts 2025, official Shell archive, accessed 2026-04-16.

## Why These Seven Work Together

The Shell set is now a coherent sequence rather than a few isolated inflection points. 2014-2016 show baseline, portfolio choice, and integration. 2020-2022 show crisis, governance/identity change, and energy-security disruption. 2024-2025 show mature execution under Wael Sawan. This supports quantitative and qualitative comparison of strategic leadership, horizon scanning, governance, option choice, and execution language.

## Why Other Candidate Years Were Not Selected

- 2005: Historically meaningful because it would take the analysis back toward Shell's earlier governance/restructuring history, but that would shift the paper away from the selected CE lens of current energy-transition renewal, portfolio choice, and ambidexterity.
- 2010: Useful as a post-financial-crisis / energy-cycle baseline, but less directly connected to the BG acquisition, Shell plc identity shift, energy-security shock, and Wael Sawan-era capital discipline that define the selected arc.
- 2021: Important transition year, but 2022 better captures the renamed Shell plc structure, energy-security shock, and CEO-transition context.
- 2023: Useful as an early Sawan strategy-reset year, but 2024 and 2025 provide a cleaner mature Sawan-era execution pair.

## Why Not Go Further Back?

The main research design is not a full corporate history of Shell. Going much further back would add historical depth but reduce comparability with Amazon, NVIDIA, and Chevron because the older Shell case would become more about governance restructuring and legacy corporate reorganization than current strategic renewal. The selected Shell years keep enough historical span to show change over time while staying close to the contemporary energy-transition, portfolio-renewal, and investor-discipline debates.

Older Shell years remain useful as archival robustness candidates. If the paper later needs a deeper historical appendix, 2005 and 2010 are defensible background years. For the main seven-letter set, 2014 is now included as the pre-BG baseline because seven slots allow a fuller baseline-choice-integration sequence without losing contemporary comparability.

## Comparability Note

For Shell, the selected years map to comparable strategic episodes: pre-renewal baseline, major portfolio choice, post-choice integration, crisis response, governance/identity and geopolitical shock, and mature execution. This makes Shell comparable to Chevron's capital-discipline and portfolio-option arc and to the tech firms' platform-renewal arcs while preserving the slower, more regulated energy-industry logic.
