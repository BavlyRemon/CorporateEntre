# NOTES ON DATA QUALITY

1. **Amazon 1997**: No 10-K available in the corpus; figures come from the shareholder letter only. Revenue ($147.8M) and headcount (614) are the only confirmed metrics.

2. **NVIDIA fiscal year**: Ends late January/early February. "FY2017" = year ended January 29, 2017; "FY2025" = year ended January 26, 2025. CEO letters cite "fiscal year" numbers; calendar year comparisons with Amazon, Shell, and Chevron require one-year offset.

3. **NVIDIA stock split**: 4-for-1 on July 19, 2021. FY2022, FY2023, and FY2025 EPS are post-split. The FY2021 filing reports pre-split EPS ($6.90 diluted); retroactively restated to $1.73 in later filings.

4. **Shell CCS vs. GAAP**: Shell's "CCS earnings" strip out inventory holding gains/losses from oil price movements. "Adjusted earnings" (from 2020) further strips impairments and restructuring. The 2020 GAAP loss of $21.7B vs. adjusted earnings of $4.8B illustrates the gap: $52.4B in non-cash impairment charges dominated the GAAP result.

5. **Shell 2016 BG acquisition**: Organic capex was $22.1B; total capex including the $52.9B BG Group acquisition was $79.9B. Net debt nearly tripled from $27B to $73B as a result.

6. **Chevron 2013 negative FCF**: The $37.9B cash capex exceeded the $35.0B operating cash flow because Gorgon, Wheatstone, and multiple deepwater projects were in peak construction phase. This was planned; FCF recovered sharply as those projects came online.

7. **Chevron vs. Shell production**: Both companies report in mboe/d but include different components (oil, gas, NGL). Direct comparison is valid as an order-of-magnitude indicator but detailed composition differs.

8. **Amazon "free cash flow"**: Amazon has changed its FCF definition multiple times. Throughout this report, FCF = operating cash flow minus PPE purchases net of proceeds, as defined in each respective filing. The 2021 and 2022 negative FCF years reflect deliberate infrastructure investment, not financial distress.
