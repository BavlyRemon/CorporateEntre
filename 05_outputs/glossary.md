## Part A — Conceptual terms

### Corpus & unit of analysis
- **Corpus** — the 28 selected CEO/stakeholder letters (7 per company × Amazon, NVIDIA, Shell, Chevron), 1997–2025, 61,318 body words.
- **Unit of analysis** — one selected leadership letter per company-year. Boundaries are recorded in `02_extracted_text/selected_ceo_letters/selected_ceo_letters_manifest.csv` (the manifest), never re-derived.
- **Body words** — word count *after* stripping the extraction header (everything before the first `\n---\n`). The tokenizer counts `[A-Za-z0-9]+(?:[-']…)?` runs.
- **Selected years (fixed)** — Amazon 1997, 2016, 2020–2022, 2024–2025 · NVIDIA 2017–2018, 2020–2023, 2025 · Shell 2014–2016, 2020, 2022, 2024–2025 · Chevron 2013, 2018, 2020–2024.

### IPM — Innovation Process Model (the five themes)
The deductive framework the dictionary is built around. Each theme is a lens; a letter is scored on all five independently (themes overlap on purpose).

1. **Strategic Leadership** — how the CEO frames their own role: founder vs. operator, decisive vs. disciplined, stewardship, accountability, boldness.
2. **Horizon Scanning / Sense-making** — how the firm names the future: discontinuity, uncertainty, technology inflections, the external/regulatory environment.
3. **Purpose, Vision, and Governance** — who the firm says it exists for: customers, shareholders, communities; mission, identity, governance, oversight.
4. **Strategic Options, Experimentation, and Choices** — how the firm spends choices: R&D, invention, partnerships, acquisitions, capital allocation, pilots, bets.
5. **Agile Execution and Organization** — how the firm gets things done: speed, adaptability, operational excellence, talent, capability, infrastructure, scaling.

- **Dominant IPM theme** — for a company (or year), the IPM theme with the highest per-1,000-word rate. NB: three of four firms share *Purpose* as dominant, but the *content* of "purpose" differs sharply (Amazon = customer obsession; Shell = energy progress/net-zero; Chevron = shareholder returns) — read the count with the text.
- **Subtheme** — finer breakdown of each IPM theme used in the report appendix (`THEME_SUBTHEMES` in `lexicon.py`), e.g. "Founder/ownership mentality" under Strategic Leadership.

### Cross-cutting paired lenses
Binary opposition pairs that run *over the top* of the five IPM themes. Each yields a **share** = first / (first + second).

| Pair | Share metric | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| **Explore vs. Exploit** | explore share | New options/invention vs. squeezing the known. The headline lens. |
| **Customer vs. Shareholder** | customer share | Who the letter addresses. |
| **Long-term vs. Short-term** | long-term share | Time-horizon language. Universal here (0.84–0.89). |
| **Entrepreneurial vs. Managerial** | entrepreneurial share | Founder/builder vs. control/process framing. |
| **Internal vs. External innovation** | internal share | Build/R&D vs. acquire/partner. |
| **Risk vs. Performance** | risk share | Threat/uncertainty vs. results language. |

- **Explore terms** (stem bases): explore, experiment, discover, pilot, emerge, option, invent, innovate, research, prototype, iterate, learn, pioneer, reinvent, disrupt, revolutionize, plus literals "new business", "R&D".
- **Exploit terms** (stem bases): operational excellence, cash generation, capital discipline, return, efficient, productive, reliable, cash flow, dividend, buyback, record production, cost, margin, safety, execute, scale.
- **Explore share** = explore / (explore + exploit), 0–1. **Interpretive class**: ≥ 0.60 *exploratory leaning*, ≤ 0.40 *exploitative leaning*, between *ambidextrous/mixed*.
- **Ambidextrous/mixed** — a letter in the 0.40–0.60 band. Empirically rare (5 of 28) and almost always a *transitional* year (founding, pandemic, CEO handover, new-business launch) — read as motion, not a steady state.

### Methodology terms
- **Stem-aware matching** — a token counts if its Porter stem matches the stem of an entry's base or any alias, minus excluded surface forms. Replaced whole-word matching on 2026-05-13.
- **Porter stemmer** — pure-Python (1980 algorithm) in `00_admin/stemmer.py`; reduces innovate/innovating/innovation/innovative → one stem.
- **Base** — the canonical form of a dictionary entry (e.g. `innovate`).
- **Alias** — an extra surface form whose stem should also count (covers cases Porter under-shoots, e.g. base `discover` + alias `discovery`).
- **Exclude** — a surface form that must *not* count even if its stem matches (false neighbours, e.g. base `decisive` excludes `decision`; `experiment` excludes `experience`).
- **Literal entry** — a multi-word phrase or punctuation-bearing token (e.g. "R&D", "day 1") matched whole-word, not stemmed.
- **`stem: false`** — opts an entry out of stemming where Porter collapses unrelated words (used for `integrity`, `values`).
- **Per-1,000-word normalisation** — all counts divided by body words × 1,000. Letters range 777→5,251 words (~7×), so raw counts would just measure verbosity; the rate measures *emphasis*.
- **Coded lexical emphasis** — what the counts are. Not unique-word totals, not sentiment, not causality. Always interpreted alongside the letter text and a financial anchor.
- **Era matrix** — per-company strategic eras with an explore/exploit diagnosis, dominant IPM theme, evidence, and interpretation (`company_era_ipm_matrix.csv`).
